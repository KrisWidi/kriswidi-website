What does a website cost at KWiDi?

From €549 excl. VAT for four pages including texts, images and legal pages. With Google profile €729, with Instagram and Facebook €1,490. All packages are on the pricing page – no small print.

Do I have to provide texts and images?

No. I write the texts and take care of images. If you have your own material, I'm happy to use it – the price stays the same.

Are there monthly costs?

Not with me. You pay once, the website is yours. Only domain and hosting run through your provider – I set both up, the running costs (usually under €10 a month) are paid by you directly.

Why not just build it myself with AI or Wix?

You can. Then you have a page – but no Google profile set up, no Instagram, no texts that sell, and no one to ask. The website is the easy part. The paths that lead to it are the work.
