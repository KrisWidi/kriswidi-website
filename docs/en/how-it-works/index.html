How clicks turn into guests.

Being visible online isn't a technical question. It's the question of whether someone walks in tomorrow. Here's the chain – and how our collaboration works.

Step 1

Get found – the Google Business Profile.

When someone types “café near me” or “holiday home Croatia sea view”, Google shows the map first. There you find businesses with a complete profile: opening hours, photos, reviews, category. Whoever has no profile, or one with three details, isn't on the map. The profile costs nothing – the work is doing it right. That's exactly what's in package 2.

Step 2

Convince – the website.

The click from the profile leads to your website. Now the guest has one question: is this for me? They want pictures, prices, a feel for the place and a way to reach you. All on the phone, in thirty seconds. A website that manages that brings enquiries. One that doesn't brings nothing – no matter how pretty.

Step 3

Stay in mind – Instagram and Facebook.

Most guests don't come at first contact. They see you, forget you, see you again – and then they come. Instagram and Facebook take care of the reunion. For that you don't need daily posting, but a profile that looks like a business and a few good posts to start. That's package 3.

Is Instagram even worth it for small businesses?

For restaurants, studios and holiday rentals: yes, clearly – because pictures sell there. For trades: usually Google is enough. I'll tell you honestly if you don't need something.

How the collaboration works.

  1. You get in touch.Via the form, in two or three sentences. I reply within 24 hours on working days.
  2. We clarify what you need.By email: which package, which content, what you already have. Then you get a quote.
  3. I build, you approve.You get a draft, say what fits and what doesn't. We work until it's right.
  4. Everything goes live.Website, Google profile, Instagram – set up and linked. You get all access details.

Usually this takes two to three weeks – depending on how quickly you give feedback.

What do you have to provide? Almost nothing.

A few answers to my questions, your opening hours, prices – and pictures if you have them. I write the texts. I organise images if you have none. Tech is entirely my job.
