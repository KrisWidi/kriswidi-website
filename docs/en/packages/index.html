What does a website really cost?

Are prices net or gross?

Net, plus statutory VAT. Business customers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland are invoiced under the reverse-charge procedure – your accountant knows it.

What does a website cost per month?

Nothing at KWiDi. You pay once. Only domain and hosting continue with your provider.

What's the difference to Wix, Jimdo or IONOS?

There you build it yourself or get a template on subscription. With me you get an individual website with your own texts – and, if you like, Google profile and Instagram along with it. And the site is yours.

How many revision rounds are included?

We work until it fits – within the agreed scope. What that means in detail we clarify before the start.

How long does it take?

That depends on how quickly you give feedback. Usually a website is online in two to three weeks.
