Five reasons a website brings no enquiries.

  1. Nobody finds it.No Google Business Profile, no link, no page titles anyone searches for. The website exists – but only for you.
  2. Nobody understands it.The visitor needs five seconds to know: what do you do, for whom, and what should I do now? If they don't see it immediately, they're gone.
  3. It doesn't look good on the phone.Three out of four visitors come via smartphone. If the menu isn't readable there or the enquiry button is missing, you lose three out of four guests.
  4. There's no reason to trust you.No pictures of you, no reviews, no clear process. The visitor doesn't know you – the website has to change that.
  5. There's no easy way to reach you.The contact form has twelve fields, the phone number is hidden, the reservation link is missing. Every hurdle costs enquiries.

Why your website isn't found on Google.

Usually it's three things: the Google Business Profile is missing or incomplete, the pages have no titles anyone searches for, and nothing on the page tells Google what you actually stand for. All three can be fixed – without rebuilding the website.
