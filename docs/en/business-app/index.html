Rota

Plan: drag the assignments, done.

Open services on the right, your staff on the left. Drag, drop, the app calculates hours and shows conflicts. Week view for the overview, demand view for sites with fixed times, suggestion for a quick start.

  • Target times per site. Pool on Mondays, garden on Thursdays – the app reminds you.
  • Absences included. Leave and sick days block automatically.
  • Everyone sees their day. On their phone, with address and tasks.
Time tracking

Clock in: in, break, out.

One button, three states. Times flow into the monthly overview per employee, corrections go to you as a request. At month end you export an Excel for payroll – in Spain in the format of the working-time register under Art. 34.9 ET.

  • No paper, no recalculating. Hours, breaks, overtime – automatic.
  • Export for your accountant. Monthly, as Excel or PDF.
  • Any language. You plan in English, every employee reads the app in their own language – Russian, Spanish, Arabic, whatever they need.
Reports

Report: checklist, photos, send.

Every service has checklist items from the builder – pool cleaned, filter checked, chlorine 1.2 mg/l. The employee ticks, photographs, adds a remark. You review, approve, the customer receives the report by e-mail – with your logo, as PDF.

  • Workflow with approval. Draft, submitted, returned, approved, sent.
  • Report templates in your design. Set up by me during onboarding.
  • Readings as a history. Chlorine, pH, temperature, meter readings – per site over months.

Frequently asked questions

What do I get with Custom for €4,695?

An app that contains only what your business needs – and the way you work: custom modules and fields, workflows and approvals by your rules, reports in your design, your languages, data migration from your current tool. The monthly price is the same as Standard.

How many employees is the app for?

From one to about 50. One employee is included in the monthly price, each additional one is €9 per month. From 25 employees we agree a custom price.

What about statutory time tracking?

The app keeps a working-time register with in, break and out per employee, exportable monthly. The register follows the Spanish Art. 34.9 ET; whether it is sufficient for your business and country is something to confirm with your accountant or lawyer – I set up what you need.

Can I bring existing data?

Yes. I import staff, customers and sites from Excel or another tool. That is part of the setup.

What happens if I cancel?

You cancel at month end, export your data beforehand – times, reports, customers – and that's it. No trailing costs.

Can every employee see everything?

No. Employees see their day, their hours and their reports. You and your office staff see everything. We define who has which rights during setup.

Does the app work offline?

Clocking in and reporting need a connection – mobile data is enough. Photos upload as soon as the connection is back.

Who is behind the app?

The software is called Jornado and is continuously developed. I set it up for your business, support you and am your contact – you don't have to deal with anything.

Can I get the app together with a website?

Yes, and that is the best combination: the website brings customers, the app organises the work. Booked together, the app setup fee is waived.
